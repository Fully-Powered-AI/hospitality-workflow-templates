# Fully Powered Workflow Templates: Hospitality

A house that runs ahead of the guest. These workflow templates are written for hotels, restaurants and venues. They prepare the lists, options and drafts behind a smooth service from your own bookings and records, and the manager decides what changes and what is sent.

This repository holds 6 workflow templates for hotel, restaurant and venue managers. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The workflow templates

- **[Group room block pickup review](group-block-pickup-review/)**: As a group's room block nears its review or release date, the revenue manager and group-sales owner get one brief: rooms blocked, rooms confirmed, room nights still unfilled, counts that do not reconcile, and the questions for the account owner. A person decides any release or revised offer; the brief releases nothing.
- **[Menu input cost change review](menu-input-cost-change-review/)**: When supplier prices change, the chef and food-and-beverage manager get one worksheet: each price movement tied to the quantities in the supplied recipes, the cost per portion of every affected dish before and after, and questions for the chef. It sets no menu price.
- **[Public guest review reply](public-guest-review-reply/)**: Answer an online guest review without exposing the guest's stay. Hand over the review and the service actions you have confirmed, and get a short public reply plus separate private notes, each statement tied to its source. The guest relations manager checks and approves.
- **[Recurring repair service history](recurring-repair-service-history/)**: When repeated maintenance keeps disturbing guests, the facilities manager gets one brief by equipment and room area: repeated work orders, confirmed service interruptions and missing inspection records side by side. It finds no cause and orders no work.
- **[Room turn priority list](room-turn-priority-list/)**: Each morning the housekeeping supervisor gets one worklist of the rooms to turn, in the property's own priority order, with rooms awaiting inspection marked and every conflict in status written as a question. It inspects nothing and assigns nobody.
- **[Service roster options](service-roster-options/)**: For a department manager building a rota for expected occupancy, covers or events: two or three roster options side by side, with each role's need, the staff confirmed available, the periods left uncovered and the labor-cost assumptions you supplied. You verify and approve; nothing is published.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

To take the whole collection, press **Use this template** at the top of this page: it copies every folder into a repository in your own account, yours to change.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What SKILL.md is

Each folder holds a file named `SKILL.md`. It is the technical file: the same instructions and output format as the template, in the packaged form that AI products which load instructions from a folder read. It is generated from `TEMPLATE.md`, so the two always agree. The workflow template is the whole folder; `SKILL.md` is one way of loading it. If your AI product does not read that format, use `TEMPLATE.md`.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
