# Public guest review reply: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Hotel Q is an invented 40-room hotel, the guest is invented, and so are the people, the dates and every record below. First come the inputs the guest relations manager supplied, in full, then the draft the capability returned.

## The inputs

```
REVIEW
Site: Review site P. Posted Sunday 8 November 2026. Rating: 3 of 5.
Name shown: TravellerR

"Lovely location and the breakfast was the best part of the stay. The
room was noisy on our first night because of building work outside, and
the lift was out of order on the day we left, which was hard with our
bags. The front desk did move us to a quieter room for the second night,
which we appreciated. Wish we had been warned about the building work
when booking."

VERIFIED SERVICE ACTIONS
Ref | Action | Confirmed by | Date confirmed | May be mentioned publicly
A1 | Front desk moved a guest to a quieter room on request | Duty manager | 2026-11-07 | yes
A2 | Passenger lift repaired and back in service | Maintenance lead | 2026-11-09 | yes
A3 | Reservations now add a note about known building work to pre-arrival messages | Reservations manager | 2026-11-09 | no
A4 | One night's room charge refunded to the reviewing guest's card | Finance | 2026-11-09 | no

RESPONSE GUIDANCE (RG-1, dated 1 October 2026)
Voice: warm, plain, first person for what I did, "we" for the hotel.
Greeting: "Hello" and the name shown on the review.
Length: at most 100 words including the sign-off.
No exclamation marks. No "unfortunately".
Contact route to offer: guest relations, through the contact page on our
website. Never an email address or a phone number.
Sign-off: Dana, Guest Relations Manager, Hotel Q
Manager: Dana. Tell after posting: duty manager, marketing.

PLATFORM LIMITS
None.
```

## The output

```
PUBLIC GUEST REVIEW REPLY: Review site P, posted Sunday 8 November 2026, 3 of 5
Status: Draft for Dana. Not posted. Dana checks accuracy and the guest's privacy before approving.
Sources: the review; actions A1 to A4 as supplied; RG-1, dated 1 October 2026.

WHAT THE REVIEW RAISED
R1. Praise: the location and the breakfast. Thanked in the reply.
R2. Complaint: noise on the first night from building work outside. Acknowledged with an apology; no action listed.
R3. Complaint: the lift out of order on the day of departure. Acknowledged with an apology and answered by A2.
R4. Praise: the front desk moved the guest to a quieter room for the second night. Acknowledged, supported by A1.
R5. Wish: to have been warned about the building work at booking. Not answered in public: the only related action, A3, may not be mentioned publicly. See the private notes.

PUBLIC REPLY (97 words, limit 100)
Hello TravellerR, thank you for your review and for the kind words about our location and breakfast. I'm sorry the building work made your first night noisy and that the lift was out of order on the day you left. I'm glad our front desk could move you to a quieter room for the second night. The lift has since been repaired and is back in service. If you would like to talk with us about your stay, please contact our guest relations team through the contact page on our website.
Dana, Guest Relations Manager, Hotel Q

WHERE EACH SENTENCE COMES FROM
1. "Hello TravellerR, thank you for your review and for the kind words about our location and breakfast." Greeting and thanks from RG-1; R1.
2. "I'm sorry the building work made your first night noisy and that the lift was out of order on the day you left." R2, R3.
3. "I'm glad our front desk could move you to a quieter room for the second night." R4; A1.
4. "The lift has since been repaired and is back in service." A2, which may be mentioned publicly.
5. "If you would like to talk with us about your stay, please contact our guest relations team through the contact page on our website." Contact route from RG-1.
6. Sign-off: RG-1.

PRIVATE FOLLOW-UP NOTES (not for posting)
Open points: R5. The guest wishes they had been warned at booking. A3 is confirmed, but it is not marked for public mention. Dana decides whether the reply may say anything about it.
Confirmed actions kept out of the public reply: A3, not marked for public mention. A4, not marked for public mention; the reply offers and hints at no compensation.
Decisions for Dana: 1. Whether to mention A3 publicly. 2. Whether anything about A4 is said to the guest privately; this capability does not draft that message.
Claims in the review that conflict with a confirmed action: none.
Serious allegations: none.
Names to handle: none (no staff, guest or child is named; "TravellerR" is the name shown).
Tell after posting: duty manager, marketing.

LEFT OUT OF THE PUBLIC REPLY
Dates and length of stay, room details, the rate and any charge or refund, how the stay was booked, who travelled, staff names, and any action not marked for public mention. The only stay details in the reply are the ones the review states: the first night, the second night, the building work, the lift and the quieter room.

Draft for Dana's approval. Nothing has been posted, sent or recorded.
```
